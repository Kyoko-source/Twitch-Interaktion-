Exactly a perfectly aligned 2 by 2 grid of equally sized SQUARE paintings, each goes edge to edge in its quadrant, no gutters, no frames, no perspective, no lettering. TOP RIGHT: little old red toy train winding through a muted forest and hills, vintage illustrated storybook gouache. BOTTOM LEFT: pale whale drifting among dusky teal clouds and a few faded coral balloons, wistful empty children's dream painting. BOTTOM RIGHT: an olive crocodile sleeping beside a dark pond under flowering reeds, unmistakable long snout. All four share professional tactile gouache brushwork on aged cracked canvas, muted dusty ochre, sage, dirty cream, desaturated teal and faded coral, unsettling nostalgia rather than cartoon clipart, physically plausible old daycare artwork. Very subtle stains, fine craquelure, scuffs, darkened canvas edges inside each quadrant. Subjects clearly readable but no glaring white patches. Flat even neutral lighting: no baked cast shadows or directional spotlights. No 3D render, no people, no typography, no watermark. Layout exactly FOUR equal squares, 2 columns by 2 rows, sharp quadrant boundaries at 50 percent.
