# v1.7.0 asset provenance

Mode: built-in image_gen, two original raster assets. The selected outputs were
copied into the project unchanged, preserving the clown portrait transparency.
The meshes, signs, audio and material shaders are project-authored code.
The window watcher's non-graphic dark red smears are a procedural game shader.

## Clown

Saved: `assets/annex/clown-face.png`

Production game texture: one original frightening adult circus clown HEAD ONLY, perfectly front facing orthographic, centered on a TRUE TRANSPARENT background, no neck, shoulders or hands. Tall gaunt human face, believable human proportions and small watchful eyes, aged cracked dirty ivory face paint, asymmetric worn burgundy clown lip makeup, dark charcoal tears beneath the eyes, faded ochre small nose makeup, sparse tangled auburn side hair, furrowed brow, uncanny closed crooked smile. This is the exhausted sinister cook of an abandoned indoor children's playground. Strong grounded psychological horror, not cartoon, emoji, cute toy, recognizable franchise character or celebrity. NO blood, gore or injuries. Fine skin pores and dry greasepaint detail. Even neutral diffuse frontal albedo lighting, no harsh side shadows or baked spotlight, to map onto a curved 3D facial mesh. Entire head inside image with transparent margin; about 85 percent image height. No hat, environment, labels, border or text.

## Krokodilhaut

Saved: `assets/creatures/croc-scales-v3.png`

Production physically based game material albedo tile, seamless repeatable 1:1 square, top-down orthographic no perspective. An old realistic dark olive crocodile-pattern PVC coating on an abandoned giant inflatable playground attraction: small irregular interlocking reptile scutes and fine pebbled scales, tiny shallow molded pores and subtly cracked worn pigment, patches of faded moss green and dirty ochre, grubby age. Fine believable leathery rubber rather than real wet animal skin, small scale detail consistent throughout. Neutral flat diffuse albedo lighting without baked shadows, highlights, seams, large objects or decorative patterns. Photorealistic material scan quality. No face, eyes, teeth, creature, text or border. Opaque edge-to-edge texture; repeats seamlessly on both axes.
