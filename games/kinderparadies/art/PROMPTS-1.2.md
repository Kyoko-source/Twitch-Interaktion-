## Exact prompt

Production seamless albedo texture for an actual 3D plush rabbit toy in a realistic abandoned indoor playground horror game. Dense short worn beige-brown teddy-bear plush fabric, matted tiny fibers, irregular flattened bald spots exposing woven backing, faint dirty gray smudges, natural uneven fiber clumps. Perfectly flat macro material scan, evenly diffuse lit, no directional lighting, no glossy shine, no perspective, no object silhouette, no face, no seams, no text. Tileable left/right top/bottom. Warm desaturated oatmeal taupe, predominantly light midtone rather than black. Fine fiber detail at 2K quality; material should convincingly read as old soft fabric when mapped onto a sculpted stuffed toy, not concrete, leather, plastic or a flat beige rectangle. No composition or room scenery, this is a real production surface texture.

Other production textures/models use verified CC0 Poly Haven sources; see
`assets/polyhaven-provenance.json`. Sculpture, sewn curves, drape folds, furniture
and cake are authored meshes generated by `tools/model-nachtwache-heroes.py`.
Optical falloff, fog density, wet-floor response and rope meshes are code-native
3D/physical effects. They do not substitute a concept image for playable art.
