# PEPPLE Pinball assets

- `env-colorful-studio.hdr`: "Colorful Studio" HDRI by Sergej Majboroda, Poly Haven, CC0 – https://polyhaven.com/a/colorful_studio
- `walnut.jpg`: "Black Walnut Veneer 01" diffuse texture, Poly Haven, CC0, downscaled to 512 px – https://polyhaven.com/a/black_walnut_veneer_01
- `mascot.webp`: the Aviary mascot (`/assets/aviary-mascot.png`), resized for the playfield print.
- Playfield artwork, inserts, apron card, bumper caps and the DMD font are drawn in code (`src/pinball/playfield.js`, `render.js`, `dmd.js`).
