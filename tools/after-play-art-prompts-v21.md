Mode: built-in `image_gen` tool. Original generated outputs copied into the

## Newspaper photograph atlas

Saved as `games/kinderparadies/assets/story/archive-photos-v21.png`.

Create a production bitmap asset for the fictional horror game After Play. Asset: a perfectly aligned 2 by 2 contact sheet of FOUR DIFFERENT archival black-and-white newspaper photographs, each quadrant full bleed, no borders and no text or logos. Top left: a 1980s indoor children's play centre on its opening day, empty birthday table with paper hats, streamers and an unsettling rabbit mascot at the far end, shot on documentary 35mm film. Top right: the same building at night seen from outside with police tape, rain and a half-open entry door, no readable writing. Bottom left: deserted institutional kitchen with a huge stained stockpot, an abandoned clown mask on the counter, harsh flash photography. Bottom right: the sealed, decaying entrance of a forgotten play centre, piles of paper and a cracked window, ominous soft fog. All fictional, no identifiable real people or actual victims, no children shown. Photorealistic archival photography, deep textured blacks and dusty midtones, film grain, subtle halftone printing, scratched negative, tangible material detail. Each quadrant equally sized and compositionally separate. Do not include newspaper layouts, captions, headlines or paper outside the photos; these photos will be inserted into German newspaper pages by code.

## Party-room mural

Saved as `games/kinderparadies/assets/creatures/party-fresco-v21.png`.

Production environment texture for a high quality 3D horror game After Play: a wide rectangular wall mural painted by hand onto old cream plaster, straight-on orthographic view, no perspective and no surrounding room. A once-cheerful 1980s birthday carnival fresco, now deeply disturbing: an enormous faded harlequin clown face forms the centre of a toy theatre with drawn-back red curtains, four empty little painted chairs beneath it, twisted animal carousel creatures at both sides, crooked bunting and stars. The clown has a long narrow face, empty dark eye sockets, an unnaturally stretched painted grin, subtle cracked porcelain-like paint, no silly cartoon look. Torn areas reveal older charcoal childlike drawings under the paint. Restrained dirty ochre, petrol blue, faded burgundy and sickly ivory; age, peeling paint, water damage, hairline cracks and damp brown staining. Museum-quality tangible weathered painted surface. It must read as an ominous mural on the wall, not as live characters or a portal. No readable text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no frame. Wall art only, beautifully detailed and haunting. Wide landscape format.
